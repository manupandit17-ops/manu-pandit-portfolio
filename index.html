Two Power BI builds from my current role.

Oracle Certification Dashboard (Global and US)

Power BI · Power Query · DAX

Leaders needed one place to see certification compliance across Global and US regions. I combined certification data with employee roster data so leaders, territory managers and stakeholders can track status, spot compliance risks and plan for upcoming expirations.

What I built

  • Cleaned and combined Excel and CSV certification and roster data in Power Query, including AC vs Non-AC classification.
  • Data model and DAX measures for certification KPIs, compliance metrics and expiry tracking (30 / 60 / 90 / 90+ days).
  • Interactive views by Region, Territory, Capability and Employee, supporting automated renewal notifications.
  • Used ChatGPT, Copilot and Claude for DAX optimisation, business-logic checks and executive summaries, then published to Power BI Service.

What changed

  • A single compliance platform for Global and US operations.
  • Clearer certification status by region, territory and capability.
  • Less manual effort tracking expirations and reporting compliance.
  • Proactive renewal planning through automated expiry monitoring.
  • Higher stakeholder adoption through self-service, real-time reporting.

Oracle eOPN Catalyst Opportunity Tracking Dashboard

Power BI · Power Automate · DAX

What I built

  • Used ChatGPT, Copilot and Claude to optimise DAX, check business logic and draft summaries; published to Power BI Service for self-service use.

What changed

  • Automated opportunity tracking and more accurate reporting.
  • Real-time view of approved opportunities, incentive earnings and territory performance.
  • Shorter dashboard development time through AI-assisted DAX and report design.
